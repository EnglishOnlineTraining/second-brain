- ## One-Line Essence
	- AI search assistants parse pages into small structured chunks and assemble answers from them, so content now competes to be selected chunk-by-chunk, not ranked page-by-page — winning that requires tight title/H1/description alignment, clear headings, Q&A pairs, schema markup, and concise self-contained ("snippable") phrasing.
- ## Key Arguments
	- 1. Traditional SEO (crawlability, metadata, links) is necessary but no longer sufficient — it's the floor, not the strategy.
	- 2. Structure is the mechanism of selection: headings, Q&A blocks, lists/tables, and JSON-LD schema are what let an assistant confidently lift a piece of content into an answer.
	- 3. Specific, named anti-patterns matter more than vague "write well" advice: walls of text, hidden-tab answers, PDF-only or image-only key info, decorative punctuation, unanchored buzzwords.
