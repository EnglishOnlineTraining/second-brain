- ## Session: Migrated the vault to a Logseq graph #decision
	- **Objective:** Set up the second brain so it actually opens correctly in the Logseq app, in its own directory separate from the `vocab-games` website repo.
	- **Key Decisions**
		- **Decision:** Rebuild the vault as a native Logseq graph (`journals/`, `pages/`, `logseq/config.edn`, `assets/`) instead of the original Obsidian-style nested-folder layout.
			- *Rationale:* Logseq needs flat `pages/` files and block/outline content with `key:: value` page-properties — an Obsidian vault with YAML frontmatter and nested folders doesn't open cleanly as a Logseq graph.
			- *Linked context:* [[mem/history/system-evolution]]
		- **Decision:** Keep `raw/` outside the graph (not under `pages/`).
			- *Rationale:* it's immutable reference material, not something to browse/edit as linked notes; `[[raw/...]]` references from wiki pages still resolve fine without a backing page file.
		- **Decision:** Move `ctx/sessions/` decision logging into the daily journal (this file) instead of one page per session.
			- *Rationale:* that's the native Logseq idiom — the whole point of journals.
	- **Insights Generated**
		- Namespace pages (`wiki/concepts/x`) are encoded in the `title::` property rather than relied on for on-disk filename encoding, since that encoding has varied across Logseq versions — this makes the vault version-robust.
